- Notes on the tutorial. We need to sort out the basic and complex things in this game and show them to the player in order. My first idea is as follows. 1: Ship with front gun only - player should be explained how generator, shields and motor works. There should be some enemies that will be destroyed in the level and the player will see that shooting up lasers will take the precious energy from the generator. 2: Player should be introduced to the support system (cards). I think it would be great to have it like this - in this tutorial, there will be an enemy that will regenerate HP over time and you cannot kill it at all. Then you will get the call from support and you will pick up damage boost that will push you over the edge and you will be able to kill the regenerating enemy. Then the story narrator will tell you that support can help you in various situations and you will need to handle not only lacking damage, but lacking defense, speed or just general functions of the ship such as generator or motor. 3: Player should be introduced to the AOE weapon and side weapons. The originally locked "shop" screen would now open and the player should be navigated to buy some aoe weapon. Then with this weapon, the tutorial level would be easy to complete. 4: The player would be introduced to advanced aspects of this game - the generator, shield and motors. I think a good tutorial level would be to instruct the player to buy a better motor, but when you will start the tutorial level, your generator would not be able to generate any extra power and your weapons will be barely firing. Then a narrator tell you what the problem is and that you will need to pick up extra generator power from the support. This will again push you over the edge and you will be able to solve the level.
